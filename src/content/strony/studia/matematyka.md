  ## Specialisations

  - *data analysis* (AD) — coordinators: Prof. Małgorzata Bogdan, Dr Grzegorz Wyłupek
  - *applied mathematics* (MS) — coordinator: Prof. Grzegorz Karch
  - *actuarial and financial mathematics* (MAF) — coordinator: Dr Marek Arendarczyk
  - *theoretical mathematics* — coordinator: Dr hab. Jan Dymara
  - *mathematics teaching*

  The *mathematics in economics* specialisation is being phased out (available only to students following the previous curriculum).

  ### Choosing a specialisation

  - **Form C1** — 1st-semester bachelor's students, mathematics teaching (deadline: **31 December**)
  - [**Form C2**](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/SWEQQ1YF5L) — 4th-semester bachelor's students (deadline: **30 June**)
  - [**Form C3**](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/LKA4mEgVtN) — 1st-semester master's students (deadline: **15 October**)

  ## Teaching placements

  Teaching placements are compulsory for students of the *mathematics teaching* specialisation. See the [placement regulations (Polish)](https://www.math.uni.wroc.pl/sites/default/files/regulamin-praktyk-nauczycielskich-2026.pdf).

  ## Master's thesis

  At the end of the 2nd or the beginning of the 3rd semester, master's students choose a supervisor and agree on the topic of their thesis. By **31 October** (summer admission) or **31 March** (winter admission) they submit their choice using [**Form E**](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=-b5xKxM7MkS19B9awieNDLjzAPP4phpMtcTE0K7u9RpUNkVPUVc4SUxCTkQ5MzdSQTYyS0YxTzBHMy4u). See also [Exams and diploma](/en/studia/egzaminy-i-dyplom).

## Praca magisterska

Student studiów II stopnia pod koniec 2. semestru bądź na początku 3. semestru wybiera promotora i uzgadnia z nim tematykę pracy magisterskiej. W terminie do **31 października** (rekrutacja letnia) lub **31 marca** (rekrutacja zimowa) zgłasza wybór promotora i tematykę pracy na formularzu:

[**Formularz E**](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=-b5xKxM7MkS19B9awieNDLjzAPP4phpMtcTE0K7u9RpUNkVPUVc4SUxCTkQ5MzdSQTYyS0YxTzBHMy4u) — zgłoszenie wyboru promotora i tematyki pracy dyplomowej

Jeśli student do tego czasu nie znajdzie promotora, zgłasza wniosek o pomoc. Więcej w dziale [Egzaminy i dyplom](/studia/egzaminy-i-dyplom).
