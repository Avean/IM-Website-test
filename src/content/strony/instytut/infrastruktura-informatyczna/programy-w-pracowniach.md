  ## Software on all Linux computers

  In addition, all computers running Linux provide:

  - **basic compilers, programming tools and interpreters:** anjuta, automake, awk, ATLAS, binutils, bigloo, bison, clisp, cpp, cvs, ecj, eclipse, erlang, flex, fort77, gcc, gcl, gdb, gfortran, gij/java, glade, gnat, gpc, prolog, kdbg, make, lua, perl, php, python, qt3/qt4, quanta, ruby, tcl/tk, codeblocks, CUDA NVIDIA;
  - **mathematical software:** [FriCAS](http://axiom-wiki.newsynthesis.org/), Octave, Maxima, Axiom, GAP, Scilab, R, gnuplot, gp/PARI, GiNaC, Grace, Dr. Geo, LabPlot, Dia, KDE mathematical programs;
  - **text editors and word processors:** OpenOffice, AbiWord, KWord, LaTeX, Kile, Bluefish, gedit, Kate, nano, Emacs, Vim, sed, ed;
  - **browsers and network tools:** Firefox, IceWeasel, Konqueror, Epiphany, links, lynx, curl, wget, Chromium;
  - **other programs:** GNOME, KDE, bash, GIMP, K3b, man pages, tar, gzip, iconv, mc, coreutils, xpdf, enscript, aspell, bc, ImageMagick, Kaffeine, Xvfb;

  as well as other standard Unix operating system commands.

## Programy na wszystkich komputerach z systemem Linux

Na wszystkich komputerach pod systemem Linux dostępne są ponadto:

- **podstawowe kompilatory, narzędzia programistyczne, interpretery:** anjuta, automake, awk, ATLAS, binutils, bigloo, bison, clisp, cpp, cvs, ecj, eclipse, erlang, flex, fort77, gcc, gcl, gdb, gfortran, gij/java, glade, gnat, gpc, prolog, kdbg, make, lua, perl, php, python, qt3/qt4, quanta, ruby, tcl/tk, codeblocks, CUDA NVIDIA;

- **edytory i procesory tekstu:** OpenOffice, AbiWord, KWord, LaTeX, Kile, Bluefish, gedit, Kate, nano, Emacs, Vim, sed, ed;
- **przeglądarki i programy sieciowe:** Firefox, IceWeasel, Konqueror, Epiphany, links, lynx, curl, wget, Chromium;
- **inne programy:** GNOME, KDE, bash, GIMP, K3b, strony podręcznika (man), tar, gzip, iconv, mc, coreutils, xpdf, enscript, aspell, bc, ImageMagick, Kaffeine, Xvfb;
